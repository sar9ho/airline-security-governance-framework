# SkyShield Airlines – Risk Heatmap Legend

Heatmap colors are mapped to Residual Risk severity:

| Color | Range | Meaning |
|--------|--------|----------|
| 🟢 Green | 0–5 | Low residual risk |
| 🟡 Yellow | 6–12 | Medium residual risk |
| 🔴 Red | 13–25 | High residual risk |

Plotting Coordinates:
- X-axis = Likelihood (1 = Rare, 5 = Almost Certain)
- Y-axis = Impact (1 = Minimal, 5 = Catastrophic)
- Each risk plotted as (Likelihood, Impact)

Visual goal:
Top-right = highest concern  
Bottom-left = lowest concern
