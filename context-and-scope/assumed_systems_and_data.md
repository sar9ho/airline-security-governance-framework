# SkyShield Airlines – Systems & Data Scope

## In-Scope Systems
1. Public website & booking platform
2. Mobile app and loyalty rewards system
3. Corporate identity & email platform
4. HR & payroll system
5. Airport & operations technology (OT-like systems)
6. Third-party vendors for payments and customer analytics

## Data Types
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Payment card information (PCI)
- Employee and contractor data
- Operational and system logs
- Flight schedules and operations data

## Data Classification Model
1. Public
2. Internal Use
3. Confidential
4. Restricted (Highly Sensitive)
