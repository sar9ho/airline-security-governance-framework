# SkyShield Airlines – Threat Landscape

Airlines face unique cyber threats targeting digital, operational, and human systems. Relevant threat categories include:

- Credential stuffing on customer loyalty accounts
- Phishing & social engineering against employees
- Ransomware targeting corporate IT
- Insider misuse of privileged access
- Third-party vendor breaches
- Airport OT disruption affecting ground operations

Primary threat actors include:
- Organized cybercrime groups
- Financially motivated attackers
- Nation-state and APT groups targeting aviation
