# SkyShield Airlines — Data Privacy Policy

## 1. Purpose
To protect personal data handled by SkyShield Airlines and to ensure compliance with applicable privacy regulations including GDPR, CCPA, and relevant aviation data governance laws

## 2. Scope
Applies to all personal data belonging to customers, employees, partners, and end-users processed by SkyShield systems or vendors, regardless of platform or geography

## 3. Definitions
- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual
- **Processing**: Collection, storage, use, sharing, or deletion of personal data
- **Data Subject**: The individual whose personal data is being processed

## 4. Core Principles

SkyShield adheres to the following privacy principles:

| Principle | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Lawfulness, Fairness, Transparency | Users informed via privacy notices |
| Purpose Limitation | Use only for specific legitimate business purposes |
| Data Minimization | Collect only what is strictly required |
| Accuracy | Keep data accurate & updateable |
| Storage Limitation | Retain only for defined business/legal periods |
| Integrity & Confidentiality | Protect via security and access controls |

## 5. Data Subject Rights
Where legally applicable, individuals may request:

- Access to their data (DSAR)
- Rectification of incorrect data
- Erasure (“right to be forgotten”)
- Restriction of processing
- Data portability
- Objection to certain processing
- Withdrawal of consent

Requests must be acknowledged within **72 hours** and fulfilled within **30 days**, unless legally restricted

## 6. Retention & Deletion

| Data Type | Retention Period | Disposition |
|------------|------------------|-------------|
| Airline customer booking data | 7 years | Secure deletion |
| Employee HR records | Duration of employment + 5 years | Secure archive |
| Security logs | 12–24 months | Secure overwrite or delete |

## 7. Data Sharing & Transfer
- Transfers outside the original jurisdiction require contractual safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses)
- Vendors must comply with SkyShield's Vendor Risk Management Policy

## 8. Breach Notification
- Must follow the Incident Response Policy
- Impacted data subjects must be notified where legally required
