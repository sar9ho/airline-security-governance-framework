# Access Control Policy
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Security Engineering  
**Standard Alignment:** NIST CSF PR.AC, ISO 27001 Annex A.9  

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines access control requirements for SkyShield Airlines to ensure access to systems and data is authorized, appropriate, monitored, and revoked when no longer required.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, systems, cloud platforms, and third parties accessing SkyShield information systems.

## 3. Policy Statements
- AC-01: Access must be provisioned based on least privilege and job role requirements.
- AC-02: All privileged accounts must require MFA.
- AC-03: Shared accounts are prohibited unless approved by the Security Director and logged with compensating controls.
- AC-04: Access must be removed within 24 hours following role change or termination.
- AC-05: All IAM systems must log authentication, authorization, and privilege escalation events.
- AC-06: Production access must require documented business justification.

## 4. Access Review Requirements
- Quarterly for privileged accounts  
- Annually for standard accounts  
- Immediate review following security incidents  

## 5. Violations
Violations may result in security incident handling, disciplinary action, or access removal.
