# Vendor Risk Management Policy
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Third-Party Risk Lead  
**Alignment:** NIST CSF ID.SC, ISO 27001 A.15  

## 1. Purpose
To ensure third-party risks are assessed, monitored, and managed according to business impact and data classification.

## 2. Scope
All vendors, SaaS providers, partners, and managed services.

## 3. Risk Tiering

| Tier | Criteria | Requirements |
|------|----------|--------------|
| Low | No PII or critical functions | Basic contract |
| Medium | Internal or Confidential data | Questionnaire + MFA |
| High | Restricted data or ops dependency | SOC2/ISO proof + Pen test report |
| Critical | Safety/PII regulatory impact | Onsite review + BCP verification |

## 4. Policy Requirements
- VRM-01: Vendor risk assessment required before onboarding.
- VRM-02: Renewal review required annually.
- VRM-03: Breach notification SLA must be ≤ 48 hours.
- VRM-04: Termination must include verified data deletion.
