# Incident Response Policy
**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Incident Response Team Lead  
**Alignment:** NIST 800-61, ISO 27001 A.16  

## 1. Purpose
To ensure SkyShield incidents are handled consistently, quickly, and with minimal business or regulatory impact.

## 2. Scope
All systems, networks, personnel, cloud environments, and third parties.

## 3. Lifecycle (NIST 800-61)
1. Preparation  
2. Detection & Analysis  
3. Containment  
4. Eradication  
5. Recovery  
6. Lessons Learned  

## 4. Severity Classifications

| Level | Description | Response Time | Notification |
|--------|--------------|----------------|--------------|
| S1 Low | Minimal risk | 4 hours | IR Team |
| S2 Medium | Limited impact | 1 hour | IR + Asset Owner |
| S3 High | PII or major outage | 30 min | Exec + Legal |
| S4 Critical | Safety impact | Immediate | CEO + Regulator |

## 5. Evidence Handling
- Chain of custody must be documented.
- Logs must be exported to secure evidence storage.

## 6. Reviews
All Medium+ incidents require post-mortem within 5 business days.
