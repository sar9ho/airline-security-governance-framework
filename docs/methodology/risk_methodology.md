# SkyShield Airlines – Cyber Risk Assessment Methodology

This document defines how cyber risks are identified, analyzed, scored, prioritized, and tracked for SkyShield Airlines. It ensures transparent, repeatable, and auditable risk decisions.

## 1. Definitions

- **Threat** — A potential cause of an unwanted event (e.g., attacker, accident, failure).
- **Vulnerability** — A weakness that may be exploited.
- **Asset Impact** — Business consequence if a threat successfully exploits a vulnerability.
- **Risk** — The measurable exposure resulting from a threat exploiting a vulnerability against an asset.

Formula used:
Risk = (Threat Likelihood) × (Business Impact)


## 2. Risk Scoring Dimensions

We measure risk across two components:

1. **Likelihood (1–5)** — Probability of exploitation occurring.
2. **Impact (1–5)** — Severity of outcome mapped to CIA values and business effects.

## 3. Likelihood Scoring Criteria

| Score | Definition |
|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Rare — Unlikely in industry |
| 2 | Uncommon — Limited occurrence |
| 3 | Possible — Credible vector exists |
| 4 | Likely — Active targeting observed |
| 5 | Almost Certain — Persistent or automated exploitation expected |

## 4. Impact Scoring Criteria

| Score | Business Effect |
|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Minimal operational disruption |
| 2 | Noticeable but recoverable interruption |
| 3 | Customer-facing disruption / financial cost |
| 4 | Major outage, safety implications, regulatory involvement |
| 5 | Catastrophic, long-term brand / safety / legal impact |

## 5. FAIR-Lite Calibration

Loss components (qualitatively estimated):

- Primary Financial Loss (fraud, outage, SLA penalties)
- Secondary Loss Events (litigation, compliance, PR damage)
- Safety / Operational Criticality
- Customer Trust Impact

## 6. Risk Rating Scale

| Score Range | Color | Category |
|--------------|--------|-----------|
| 1–5 | 🟢 Green | Low |
| 6–12 | 🟡 Yellow | Medium |
| 13–25 | 🔴 Red | High |

## 7. Risk Treatment Options

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Avoid | Eliminate activity causing risk |
| Mitigate | Apply compensating controls |
| Transfer | Cyber insurance or 3rd party |
| Accept | Decision recorded via exception |

All risk decisions must be documented and reviewed annually.
